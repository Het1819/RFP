# First Paid Pilot Campaign Operating Checklist

This checklist defines the end-to-end operational steps to execute our first paid pilot outreach campaign.

---

## 1. Phase 1: Pre-Campaign & Research Readiness

- [ ] **Research Batch 1:** Identify 20 target accounts using lawful, public sources. Log details in `FIRST_20_ACCOUNT_BATCH_TEMPLATE.csv`.
- [ ] **Identify Target Buyers:** Document the specific Proposal Directors, CTOs, and Capture Managers.
- [ ] **Document Pain Hypotheses:** Map specific proposal bottlenecks (manual matrix creation vs SME drafting delays) for each account.
- [ ] **Run Pre-Outreach Go/No-Go Gate:** Disqualify accounts with strict air-gapped security policies or classified data requirements.

---

## 2. Phase 2: Message QA & Outreach Execution

- [ ] **Perform Outbound QA Review:** Check messages against `OUTBOUND_QA_CHECKLIST.md` (no fake SOC 2 claims, no guaranteed win rates, include opt-out notices).
- [ ] **Execute Sales Sequence Cadence:** Send LinkedIn connection requests and follow up with Day 1-10 email sequences.
- [ ] **Honor Opt-Outs:** Process any unsubscribe or opt-out requests within 1 business day.
- [ ] **Update CRM Tracker:** Log touches and transition stages in `CRM_PIPELINE_TEMPLATE.csv`.

---

## 3. Phase 3: Meeting Prep & Evidence Capture

- [ ] **Prepare Staging Demo Environment:** Verify the staging server is running and demo data is seeded.
- [ ] **Capture Discovery Meeting Evidence:** During the call, capture evidence in `DISCOVERY_MEETING_EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md` (volume, hours, budget signal, user quotes).
- [ ] **Document WTP Evidence:** Note specific indicators of willingness to pay (e.g. positive response to the $5,000 flat pilot fee).
- [ ] **Fill Discovery Scorecard:** Calculate the qualification score. Only book demos for deals with scores >= 28/35 (GO).

---

## 4. Phase 4: Opportunity Review & Proposal Conversion

- [ ] **Draft Opportunity Memo:** Fill out the `PILOT_OPPORTUNITY_REVIEW_MEMO.md` for qualified deals.
- [ ] **Complete Pilot Deal Review Checklist:** Verify all checklist requirements (buyer identified, success metrics agreed, security response pack accepted) are ticked.
- [ ] **Execute Pilot Proposal:** Send the proposal template and schedule the legal review meeting.
- [ ] **Run Weekly Campaign Cadence:** Conduct pipeline reviews and weekly campaign report logs on Friday afternoon.
