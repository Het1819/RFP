# RFP Architect - Weekly Sales Operating Cadence

This document establishes the weekly operational routine, calendar blocks, and performance review metrics for the RFP Architect sales team.

---

## 1. Weekly Schedule & Operational Blocks

* **Monday (09:00 - 10:30) | Pipeline Review & Target Setting:**
  * Audit active CRM stages from `TARGET` to `PAID_PILOT_WON`.
  * Set weekly outreach goals (e.g. 50 new target accounts contacted).
  * Review open security/legal blockers on pipeline deals.
* **Daily (10:00 - 12:00) | Outbound & Sequence Execution:**
  * Execute Day 1-10 sequence tasks (emails, LinkedIn touches, calls).
  * Update target account list priority scores.
  * Process opt-out requests within the 1-day SLA.
* **Wednesday (14:00 - 15:00) | Mid-Week Deal Review:**
  * Review completed discovery calls and qualification scorecards.
  * Prep demo environments for booked product walkthroughs.
  * Follow up on pilot proposals currently undergoing customer legal review.
* **Thursday (15:00 - 16:00) | Pilot Health Audit:**
  * Inspect active staging pilot tenants.
  * Review user feedback submissions (`/feedback` POST events).
  * Ensure SLA response times on active blocker bugs are met.
* **Friday (14:00 - 15:30) | Weekly Metrics & Retrospective:**
  * Document weekly metrics against targets.
  * Categorize top customer objections and update objection guides.
  * Lock next week's priority accounts.
