# RFP Architect - Pilot Deal Review Checklist

Before presenting a final paid pilot proposal to a qualified prospect, the account owner must complete and document this deal review checklist.

---

## 1. Customer Scoping & Alignment

- [ ] **Economic Buyer Identified:** Confirm the VP of Sales, CTO, or COO has been identified as the budget owner and pilot signer.
- [ ] **Technical Champion Engaged:** A Proposal Lead or Capture Manager is actively championing the pilot execution.
- [ ] **RFP Pain Confirmed:** Specific bottlenecks (e.g. manual matrix Excel copying, SME drafting delays) are documented with hourly overhead estimates.
- [ ] **Paid Pilot Package Selected:** The customer has selected either the Accelerated (2-week), Standard (4-week), or Enterprise (6-week) structure.
- [ ] **Pilot Volume Aligned:** Confirm mock RFP counts and active seat numbers match the package parameters.

---

## 2. Security, Compliance, & Legal Scoping

- [ ] **Customer Data-Handling Notice Reviewed:** The customer's compliance lead has reviewed the transit and zero-retention API flow.
- [ ] **Security Questionnaire Completed:** The customer has received and signed off on our [Security Response Pack](file:///D:/RFA/Project/rfp-architect-mvp/SECURITY_RESPONSE_PACK.md).
- [ ] **No Certification Clarified:** The customer has explicitly acknowledged that the staging pilot instance is not SOC 2 or HIPAA audited.
- [ ] **Legal Review Scheduled:** Both parties have agreed to submit the pilot proposal to legal counsel for review prior to sign-off.

---

## 3. Commercial & Implementation Alignment

- [ ] **Pricing Range Accepted:** The flat pilot fee ($2,500, $5,000, or $10,000) has been discussed and accepted in writing.
- [ ] **Success Metrics Agreed:** The customer has agreed to the target gates (Recall >= 90%, CSAT >= 4.0, drafting time cut by 30%).
- [ ] **Implementation Owner Assigned:** The customer has designated a technical lead to manage user seat provisioning and onboarding.
- [ ] **Data Deletion Protocol Approved:** The customer has reviewed the exit protocol (erasure of staging DB and files).
