# RFP Architect - Account Research Worksheet & Guide

This worksheet establishes the research process, allowed/disallowed sources, and qualification gates before launching outreach to a target account.

---

## 1. What to Research Before Outreach

To ensure personalization is specific and pain-driven, research the following dimensions:
1. **Industry Segment:** Systems Integrators, IT Services, Federal/Defense Contractors.
2. **RFP/Proposal Volume signals:** How often do they bid on public solicitations? Look at award histories.
3. **Services Offered:** Cloud consulting, cyber auditing, custom software delivery (requires heavy compliance sheets).
4. **Likely Buyer Roles:** Identify Proposal Directors, VP of Sales, CTOs, and Capture Managers.
5. **Security/Data Sensitivity:** Do they require FedRAMP/ITAR, or are they standard enterprise cloud-native users?
6. **Recent Trigger Events:** New large contract wins, executive hiring for proposal teams, peak bidding quarters.

---

## 2. Allowed vs Disallowed Research Methods

### Allowed Research Sources (Lawful & Public):
* **Company Website:** Reviewing public services, case studies, and compliance pages.
* **Public Procurement Portals:** Researching award histories and active bids (e.g. SAM.gov or state registers).
* **Press Releases:** Documenting new contract wins or executive leadership announcements.
* **LinkedIn Company Pages:** Manual inspection of employee counts and job roles (no automation or scraping tools).
* **Public Job Postings:** Looking for job descriptions containing "RFP Manager" or "Proposal Coordinator" to signal volume.

### Disallowed Research (Strict Red Lines):
* **No Automated Scraping:** Do not use automated bots, browser extensions, or scraping tools to export employee emails or phone numbers.
* **No Private Data Brokers:** Do not purchase unverified lead lists containing personal contact data.
* **No Bypassing Login Walls:** Do not bypass registration walls or access confidential customer databases.
* **No Personal Sensitive Data:** Never collect or log home addresses, private mobile numbers, or personal details.

## 4. Pre-Outreach Go/No-Go Gate

* **GO:** Account segment matches ICP, volume is estimated at >=2 RFPs/month, and cloud AI terms are likely acceptable.
* **NO-GO:** Account has strict classified/defense data restrictions, requires on-premises/air-gapped staging setups, or has no active proposal volume.
