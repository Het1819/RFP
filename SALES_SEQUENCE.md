# RFP Architect - 10-Day Sales Sequence & Outbound Process

This document defines the outbound communication cadence, channels, and opt-out rules for our 10-business-day sales sequence.

---

## 1. 10-Day Cadence Schedule

| Day | Channel | Action | Message Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | LinkedIn | Send connection request + short intro note | Establish profile visibility |
| **Day 2** | Email | Send **Cold Email 1** (Focus: compliance matrix pain) | Introduce matrix extraction solution |
| **Day 4** | LinkedIn | Send **LinkedIn Follow-up** (if accepted) | Direct message touch |
| **Day 6** | Email | Send **Follow-up 1** (Focus: SME drafting delays) | Focus on SME time-saving value |
| **Day 8** | Phone / Call | Call target buyer direct line | Brief verbal touch to book discovery |
| **Day 10** | Email | Send **Follow-up 2** (Break-up / referral request) | Final check and referral ask |

---

## 2. Sequence Stop Conditions

Immediately **halt** the sequence if the prospect:
1. **Replies:** Any response (positive or negative) halts the automated sequence; transition to manual response.
2. **Opt-out Request:** If they ask to be removed or unsubscribed, record the opt-out in the target list and stop all outreach.
3. **Books Call:** Discovery call booked in the pipeline.

---

## 3. Opt-out & Compliance Rules

* **Unsubscribe Notice:** Every outreach email must include a clear, respectful option to opt-out:
  > *"If you would prefer not to receive further updates on proposal tools, please reply with 'Opt out' and we will remove your address from our lists."*
* **Honor immediately:** Opt-out requests must be processed within 1 business day. Never contact an opted-out address again.
* **No Scraped Personal Info:** Use only publicly listed corporate emails or professional profiles (LinkedIn). Do not purchase unverified personal email lists.

---

## 4. Disqualification Criteria

During the sequence, immediately **disqualify** the account if:
* They indicate they do not respond to solicitations or RFPs.
* They have a strict security policy prohibiting all external cloud AI tools (e.g. defense contractors restricted to on-prem air-gapped systems).
* They lack Subject Matter Experts to participate in the pilot validation.

*If disqualified due to security policy, send the referral template to ask for introductions to mid-sized consulting firms with standard cloud policies.*
