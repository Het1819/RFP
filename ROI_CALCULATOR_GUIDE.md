# RFP Architect - ROI Calculator & Value Model Guide

This guide helps proposal teams and capture managers model the return on investment (ROI) and cost savings generated by using RFP Architect.

---

> [!WARNING]
> **IMPORTANT:** All ROI models and savings calculations are estimates. Customers must validate these assumptions with their own historical workload data and actual hourly loaded resource rates.

---

## 1. Input Variables for the ROI Model

To calculate customized business value, gather the following metrics from the customer:
1. **RFPs / Month ($N$):** Number of proposal solicitations responded to per month.
2. **Average Drafting Hours ($H_{draft}$):** Number of hours spent manually drafting responses per RFP (primarily SME time).
3. **Hourly Loaded SME Cost ($C_{hour}$):** Fully loaded hourly cost of technical and delivery Subject Matter Experts (default: $100/hour).
4. **Compliance Table Setup Hours ($H_{matrix}$):** Hours spent manually copy-pasting requirements into Excel to build the compliance matrix (default: 4 hours/RFP).
5. **Win Rate ($W_{rate}$):** Customer's current win rate percentage.
6. **Proposal Optimization Rate ($S_{time}$):** Percentage of time saved by AI-assisted drafting (default: 30%).
7. **Monthly Subscription / Pilot Cost ($C_{tool}$):** Flat tool cost.

---

## 2. ROI Formulas & Key Outputs

### Output A: Time Saved (Hours/Month)
$$\text{Time Saved} = N \times \left( (H_{matrix} \times 0.75) + (H_{draft} \times S_{time}) \right)$$
*Assumes 75% savings on compliance matrix extraction and 30% savings on first-draft generation.*

### Output B: Cost Avoided (Savings/Month)
$$\text{Cost Avoided} = \text{Time Saved} \times C_{hour}$$

### Output C: Payback Period & Break-Even
$$\text{Payback Period (Months)} = \frac{\text{Initial Pilot Price}}{\text{Cost Avoided (Monthly)}}$$

---

## 3. Reference Scenarios

### Scenario A: Conservative (IT Services SME Team)
* **Inputs:** 2 RFPs/month, 20 hours drafting/RFP, 4 hours matrix setup/RFP. SME loaded cost: $100/hr. Pilot cost: $5,000.
* **Calculations:**
  * Matrix savings: 3 hours/RFP
  * Drafting savings: 6 hours/RFP
  * Total Time Saved: $2 \times (3 + 6) = 18\text{ hours/month}$
  * Monthly Cost Avoided: $18 \times \$100 = \$1,800/\text{month}$
  * **Payback Period:** **2.8 Months**

### Scenario B: Aggressive (Enterprise Government Contractor)
* **Inputs:** 5 RFPs/month, 50 hours drafting/RFP, 8 hours matrix setup/RFP. SME loaded cost: $120/hr. Pilot cost: $5,000.
* **Calculations:**
  * Matrix savings: 6 hours/RFP
  * Drafting savings: 15 hours/RFP
  * Total Time Saved: $5 \times (6 + 15) = 105\text{ hours/month}$
  * Monthly Cost Avoided: $105 \times \$120 = \$12,600/\text{month}$
  * **Payback Period:** **0.4 Months (First month break-even)**
