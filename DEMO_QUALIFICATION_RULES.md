# RFP Architect - Demo Booking & Qualification Rules

This document outlines the strict qualification policies to determine who receives a product demo versus who is routed to discovery or disqualified.

---

## 1. Demo Booking Rules (Who Gets a Demo)

We only book product demonstrations for prospects who meet **all** of the following criteria during discovery:
1. **RFP Volume:** The prospect processes at least **2 complex RFPs** per month.
2. **Sponsor Alignment:** The contact is either the decision-maker (VP Sales, Proposal Director, CTO) or a clear champion with direct access to them.
3. **Pain Confirmed:** Manual matrix creation or SME drafting is confirmed as a time/quality bottleneck.
4. **Willingness to Pay:** The prospect has confirmed they are open to a paid staging pilot if success metrics are demonstrated.

---

## 2. Discovery-Only Rules (Who Stays in Discovery)

Do **not** run a product demo, and keep the prospect in the discovery stage if:
* The contact is a junior researcher without decision-making influence.
* RFP volume is less than 1 per month (demonstrate value via static screenshot guides first).
* The prospect refuses to discuss pricing, pilot costs, or the decision process.
* The prospect has not defined what success looks like for their bid team.

---

## 3. Disqualification & Blocker Criteria

Immediately **disqualify** the lead and decline to book a demo if any of the following blockers are met:
* **Air-gapped Security Blocker:** The customer requires 100% on-premises, off-grid deployments during the pilot (not supported in MVP).
* **Data Sensitivity Blocker:** The customer must process active secret/classified government materials during the 4-week staging pilot.
* **No Cloud AI Policy:** The customer's IT guidelines strictly prohibit sending text to third-party LLM APIs under any enterprise terms.
* **No SME Allocation:** The customer is unwilling to dedicate at least 2 Subject Matter Experts to participate in review loops.
