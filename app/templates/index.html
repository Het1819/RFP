Risk Mitigation

Every RFP contains hidden risk.

Unlinked Requirement

Missed Requirements

Complex bids bury technical parameters in hundreds of pages. Overlooking one compliance check can lead to disqualification.

Outdated Answers

Copy-pasting text from unverified folders propagates stale technical specifications, introducing liability into proposal responses.

SME Bottlenecks

Subject matter experts are continually interrupted to re-validate core product statements, delaying active development schedules.

Inconsistent Final Output

Assembling proposals across disparate files risks layout corruption, missing tables, and formatting errors right before submission.

The NEEDS_EVIDENCE Safeguard

When no supporting text is found in the knowledge base, the system sets the status to NEEDS_EVIDENCE. We do not support guesswork or hallucinations—if there is no proof, human intervention is requested immediately to keep proposal bids accurate.

Human Supervision

AI accelerates. Experts decide.

Every drafted response can be reviewed against its linked source material before approval. Proposal accuracy is managed through assignment flows rather than autonomous generation.
