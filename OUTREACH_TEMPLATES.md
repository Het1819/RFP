# RFP Architect - Customer Outreach & Follow-up Templates

These templates are designed for B2B outbound customer development. They use a plain, professional tone focusing on reducing manual compliance matrix extraction and SME drafting overheads.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CLAIM SAFETY RULES:**
> * Do not guarantee win rates or ROI figures.
> * Do not claim the AI is error-free (always clarify it is a tool for human review).
> * Do not state that the product is SOC 2 certified or HIPAA compliant.

---

## 1. Outbound Email Sequences

### Cold Email 1: The Compliance Matrix Bottleneck
* **Subject:** RFP compliance matrices / [Prospect Company]
* **Body:**
```text
Hi [First Name],

Building compliance trace matrices in Excel is usually one of the most tedious parts of bidding on complex technical proposals.

We built RFP Architect to help proposal teams automate that setup process. It parses solicitations to extract requirement clauses, and retrieves grounding snippets from your company's capabilities documents to draft first passes.

We are onboarding a few systems integrators and IT services firms into our 4-week staging pilot program this month.

Are you open to a brief, 20-minute discovery call next Thursday to see if this matches your team's workflow?

Best regards,

[My Name]
RFP Architect Team
```

---

### Follow-up 1 (Day 3): Chasing SMEs for Drafts
* **Subject:** Re: RFP compliance matrices / [Prospect Company]

Would Thursday at 2 PM work for a quick call to discuss your current SME review process?
