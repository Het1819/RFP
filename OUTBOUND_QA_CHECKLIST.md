# RFP Architect - Outbound Message QA Checklist

Before sending any outbound email, LinkedIn message, or follow-up note, the sender must review the message against this QA checklist.

---

## 1. Compliance & Security Guardrail Checks

- [ ] **No Unsupported AI Accuracy Claims:** Verify the message does not claim the AI is 100% accurate, error-free, or makes human review unnecessary.
- [ ] **No Fake Compliance Certification Claims:** Confirm the message makes no claims that the tool is SOC 2 certified, HIPAA compliant, GDPR certified, FedRAMP authorized, or ISO 27001 audited.
- [ ] **No Guaranteed ROI or Win Rates:** Ensure there are no promised win-rate percentages or financial savings guarantees.
- [ ] **No Personal Sensitive Data:** The message must not reference home addresses, personal phone numbers, or private details.

---

## 2. Personalization & Tone Checks

- [ ] **Specific Pain Hypothesis:** The outreach references a specific, real workflow pain (e.g. manual Excel matrix parsing or SME response delays) rather than generic business jargon.
- [ ] **No Fabricated Personalization:** Avoid forced, fake familiarity (e.g. *"I noticed you like coffee"*). Stick to professional B2B relevance.
- [ ] **No False Urgency:** Avoid fake scarcity claims (e.g. *"Only 2 pilot spots left this week"*).
- [ ] **Relevant Buyer Role:** The recipient is a qualified persona (Proposal Director, Capture Manager, CTO, VP Sales).

---

## 3. Structure & CTA Checks

- [ ] **Clear 20-Minute CTA:** The message ends with a clear, low-friction invitation for a 20-minute discovery call (e.g. *"Are you open to a 20-minute call next week?"*).
- [ ] **Opt-Out Notice Included:** Email templates include a clear opt-out sentence (e.g. *"Reply with 'Opt out' to be removed"*).
- [ ] **Length Under Target:** The message is concise and readable within 30 seconds (less than 150 words for emails, less than 300 characters for LinkedIn connection requests).
