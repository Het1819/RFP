# RFP Architect - Discovery Call Script

Use this script during initial discovery conversations with qualified prospects.

---

## 1. The Opener (2 mins)
> "Thanks for jumping on today. I know your team is busy, especially with bids active. The goal of this call is simple: to understand how you currently manage your RFP and compliance response workflows, see if there's a fit for RFP Architect to help speed that up, and if so, lay out what a 4-week pilot looks like."

---

## 2. Current RFP Workflow & Volume (5 mins)
* **Question 1:** *"How many RFPs or technical solicitations does your team respond to in a typical month?"*
* **Question 2:** *"What is the average size of these RFPs in pages, and how many stakeholders (e.g. sales, security, delivery SMEs) write responses?"*
* **Question 3:** *"How do you currently build your compliance trace matrix? Is it a manual Excel extraction process?"*

---

## 3. Quantifiable Pain Points (8 mins)
* **Question 4 (Time/Cost Pain):** *"How much time do your Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) spend writing or reviewing drafts for each bid? Do you run into deadlines where SMEs complain about drafting?"*
* **Question 5 (Evidence & Grounding Pain):** *"Have you ever had a submission delayed or failed because of incorrect citations, old capabilities templates, or hallucinated claims?"*
* **Question 6 (Compliance & Rework):** *"What happens if a requirements compliance checker spots a discrepancy right before submission? What does that rework cycle cost?"*

---

## 4. Security & Data Scoping (5 mins)
* **Question 7:** *"What classification levels do your typical RFPs fall under? Are you bidding on public, private, or federal/defense contracts?"*
* **Question 8:** *"How does your organization review cloud SaaS applications that process RFP documents? Do you have an established security questionnaire process?"*

---

## 5. Budget, Process, & Pilot Fit (5 mins)
* **Question 9:** *"Who owns the budget for proposal tools and bid optimization software?"*
* **Question 10:** *"If we could show that we can cut proposal compliance matrix setup and drafting time by 40% in a 4-week pilot, what would the process look like to convert to a team license?"*

---

## 6. Next Steps & Close (3 mins)
> "Based on what you've shared—especially the pain of manually building Excel compliance tables and chasing delivery leads for technical drafts—you're a great fit for our Paid Pilot Program.
>
> The next step is a 20-minute tailored demo showing how RFP Architect handles a sample RFP. I can schedule that for later this week. Does Thursday at 2 PM work?"
